# Supporting Details and project relationships

Professional Supporting Details require at least one owned, confirmed Profile experience. One story may link to up to ten experiences, with one main placement for reuse. Education/coursework, jobs, projects, volunteering and skills/credentials are grouped in the shared searchable picker. Opportunity-specific motivation remains scoped to its opportunity, and unapproved answers remain editable drafts.

Historical unlinked or orphaned details retain their IDs, text, status, provenance and history. `needs_related_experience` is a derived listing/workspace flag, not a record rewrite. Such details are excluded from new AI/document context, including explicit assistant selections, until linked. New approvals cannot create standalone details. Historical approved-proposal migration remains compatible and flags unlinked content instead of silently treating it as a synthetic project.

Projects store optional `linked_employment_ids`, referencing Profile employment records rather than job postings. New associations require owned, confirmed jobs. Older update clients that omit this field preserve existing links. Historical unavailable associations remain visible for repair; project-linked supporting details cannot be reused until their destinations are valid. Project/job association never copies the story, changes job descriptions, or duplicates an accomplishment across job entries.

Interview reviews propose a coherent factual paragraph per actual experience, with an exact primary `user_quote` and optional additional `user_quotes`. Coaching instructions belong in feedback, not saved descriptions. Hypothetical case/scenario/coding solutions do not establish personal work. New reviews carry `detail_drafts_version=2`; legacy suggested coaching text is replaced in the review editor by original quoted answer excerpts. Combining legacy candidates is deterministic and free.

The existing interview approval endpoint additionally accepts `candidate_ids` and `experience_ids`, while retaining singular fields for compatibility. It validates all excerpts against the saved answer/confirmed transcript, rejects generated preparation/example text and private disclosures, and saves only on explicit confirmation. Combined saves preserve each source excerpt. Repeat and overlapping saves are protected. Account-scoped session buffers retain review edits during navigation/reload; no dialog action automatically requests AI.

Feedback keeps the latest review; Previous attempts opens a compact dialog with summaries and expandable read-only submissions/reviews. Preparation, conversation, transcripts and historical materials remain accessible. View transcript identifies the recording associated with the displayed review. Practice editors stay mounted across view changes; recordings remain temporary.

Backups add export-only markers for missing historical experience/job references, validate all live references before import, remap IDs, and remove markers during restoration. No schema migration. Preserve installed localhost, account isolation, immutable saved documents and explicit paid-action boundaries. Verification is desktop-only with fictional accounts/simulated providers.
