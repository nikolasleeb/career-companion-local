# Job cache and lifecycle

Hosted retention is fixed at 30 days after source verification. Local installations can change the installation-wide disposable-cache policy from Settings → App & storage (1–365 days). Freshness remains six hours. Reading a result, paging, filtering or importing records does not confirm a posting or extend its life.

A reliable deadline takes priority. Date-only deadlines include the whole closing day in the supplied IANA timezone, or UTC when unspecified. Active results exclude deadlines, retention expiry and confirmed closures on every request. Saved expired jobs are available through the Archived results view. Existing application states and documents are unaffected; new applications cannot start from an archived result.

Public employer feeds, community metadata and USAJOBS query coverage are reusable across accounts. Employer board coverage is independent of account filters. USAJOBS coverage keys include the actual provider keyword, location and bounded pagination shape, so matching cached jobs cannot stand in for an unfetched query. Six-hour snapshots and loop-scoped refresh locks coalesce account requests, with a 60-second refresh/failure suppression window. Stale snapshots remain labeled and are excluded from new-job alerts after 48 hours. Adzuna and AI discoveries, account queries, shortlists and reviews are never promoted into public storage.

Only a validated, complete employer board response can close an absent job. Partial pages and outages preserve prior verification times. A newer employer response can reopen a job; community metadata cannot reopen an employer-confirmed closure. Community verification uses the snapshot's generation time rather than download time. USAJOBS query pages never establish absence-based closure.

Startup backfills observations from existing public provider snapshots using their original timestamps. Imports are account-local and never seed public observations. An independent maintenance task runs at startup and hourly with no provider/model calls. It strips disposable expired job bodies and legacy result copies, removes unreferenced immutable versions and old public payloads, and retains identifiers for history and notification deduplication. Saved jobs and postings referenced by applications, resumes, analyses or AI reviews keep their evidence. Cleanup is idempotent; active-result filtering remains enforced even if cleanup is delayed.

Authenticated `GET /api/settings/job-cache` exposes the policy. `PUT` accepts a strict integer `retention_days` and is rejected server-side in hosted mode. Policy files contain no account preferences or credentials. Public cache rows are created by source adapters only, never by exports/imports or merged account results. No API key is returned by these interfaces.
