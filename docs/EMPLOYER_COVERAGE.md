US opportunities were prioritized using observed location text; this count is conservative and does not assert eligibility. Multi-industry tags describe the employer’s actual business or public-interest work. Employer-level career URLs, feed URLs, identity-check URLs, check timestamps and observed posting counts are recorded in `backend/app/employer_catalog.json`. The public ATS careers page is the employer-owned hiring destination checked against the feed identity. Counts are a snapshot and openings change.
